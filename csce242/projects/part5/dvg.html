Building the Bridge

Clarify the Dream

Write out, in vivid sensory detail, what the realized dream looks like. Not "I want to be healthy" but "I am running the Paris Marathon in under four hours, feeling the crowd."

Identify the Gap

Where are you now? Where is the dream? The gap is not discouraging — it is the map. It tells you exactly what must be built.

Break Into Goals

Work backwards from the dream. What must be true in five years? In one year? In 90 days? Each answer becomes a goal — specific, measurable, achievable within a timeframe.

Build Daily Habits

Goals are achieved through systems, not resolve. Identify the daily actions that compound toward each goal. The dream is the north star; habits are the footsteps.

Protect the Dream

Goals can fail without the dream dying. When a goal falls apart, return to the dream. Recalibrate. Find a new path. The dream is the reason.
